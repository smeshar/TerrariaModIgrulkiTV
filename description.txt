Elden Ring & D&D: Lands Between
=================================
A Terraria mod inspired by Elden Ring and Dungeons & Dragons.
Features:
- Fixed NPC locations across the Lands Between map.
- Invulnerable friendly NPCs (Souls-like style).
- Replaces standard world generation with bespoke Souls-themed biomes & dungeons.
- Custom weapons, magic and attributes using vanilla animations and sprites.
